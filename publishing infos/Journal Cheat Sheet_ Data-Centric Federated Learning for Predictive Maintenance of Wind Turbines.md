### Key Notes for Submission:
*   **Data-Centric Focus:** Journals like *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics* and *Engineering Applications of AI* are particularly receptive to "Data-Centric" paradigms and how they improve industrial outcomes.
*   **Federated Learning:** *IEEE Internet of Things Journal* and *IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid* have high interest in decentralized learning frameworks.
*   **Predictive Maintenance:** *Reliability Engineering & System Safety* is the premier venue for the "Maintenance" aspect of your work.
*   **Fee Confirmation:** All listed journals operate on a **Hybrid** or **Subscription** model. To publish for **free**, you must select the **"Subscription"** (non-Open Access) option upon acceptance.
